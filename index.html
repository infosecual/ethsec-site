ETHSEC

Ethereum's defense factory.

EthSec is an independent, non-profit security lab that points flagship AI at Ethereum's most critical code, funded or not, and turns money, compute, and expertise into found-and-fixed vulnerabilities.

Why now

The balance is shifting while we watch.

Large models are changing the offense-defense balance in real time, and attackers already use them. Defenders still hold a head start, but it is closing. They have direct access to their own code and to frontier models, and that advantage only lasts if it is used.

Frontier labs are beginning to open cyber-capable models to vetted teams for authorized defensive work. Token costs keep falling, so a serious budget now buys an enormous volume of review.

Crypto raises the stakes. The code is immutable, the attackers are anonymous, and a single bug can pay out all at once. Review capacity for the code Ethereum depends on should grow at the pace the threat does.

How it works

One continuous loop.

  1. Inventory

    Map the code that matters, so effort follows risk instead of habit.

  2. Discover

    Generate issues with routed lead generation at scale, across many lenses and many models.

  3. Validate

    Decide which are real, using ensemble refutation, reachability analysis, differential testing, and fuzzing.

  4. Disclose

    Route each confirmed issue to its owner through coordinated disclosure.

  5. Verify the fix

    Confirm the fix closed the issue, then begin again.

Argus picks the best model for each task from any provider and routes by measured recall and cost. It reviews each change as it lands. Senior researchers verify and file. Nothing ships on a model's say-so.

The ask

A two-year program, mostly compute.

EthSec is a non-profit with an initial two-year budget of about $10M in donations. Compute is the bulk of the cost, most of it frontier tokens. Donated model credits and bounty winnings recycled as compute credits offset it.

~$10Minitial two-year budget, in donations
~$6.6Mnet cash required over two years, base case
~1 yearof additional runway beyond that

Bug bounties make the effort trend toward self-funding. Winnings revolve back into compute and headcount, and they never choose what to hunt.

EthSec also acts as an allocation hub. It accepts money, model credits, or an auditor's lens, and multiplies each into far more found-and-fixed risk than the contributor could fund alone.

Compute is held as one class on purpose: frontier tokens, owned hardware, and benchmarking, reallocated on measured capability rather than a fixed ratio, because hardware against tokens and the frontier models themselves move fast.
